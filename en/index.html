Malek Norouzi

Writer and life coach

Toronto, Canada

Malek Norouzi is a writer, a life coach and a retired manager. He studied English at the University of Tehran, graduating in 1976, and has loved Persian literature all his life.

He worked in Tehran for more than four decades: first at the telecommunication company, in human resources and then as an office manager for almost twenty years; later in real estate; and finally at Iran Khodro, until he retired in 2018. Along the way he was named an outstanding manager (1985) and received an award for outstanding mentoring (1999).

Guiding other people has always been part of his work, and today he is also a life coach. His family and friends see him as a role model.

He emigrated to Canada and lives in Toronto. He writes, in Persian, about the history, customs and poetry of Lafour and Savadkouh in northern Iran, where his family comes from. His first book, A Love Story in Savadkouh (2025), is about the life and poems of Taghi Lafouri Savadkouhi, whose verses he first heard from his grandmother. A second book, Lafour: A Part of Iran's History, is in preparation.

He fills his days with long walks, reading, writing and time with his family.
